PrismHR Global · the offering at a glance

Hire, pay and support global teams with PrismHR Global

Four ways to serve your clients in more than 175 countries, on one platform, one click from PrismHR.

PrismHR Global lets you say yes when a client needs people abroad. Whether your client wants to employ someone in a country where they have no company, run payroll through a company they already have, engage contractors compliantly, or find the right person first, every path runs on the same platform, with our own entities in country and local expertise behind each one.

Employer of Record

For your clients hiring full-time talent in a country where they have no legal entity
  • PrismHR Global employs the worker through a local legal entity; your client manages the employee day to day.
  • Onboarding, payroll and benefits managed in country.
  • Employment contracts and compliance handled under local law.
EOR Core for identified talent  ·  EOR Plus adds sourcing, recruiting and hiring  ·  EOR Premium adds managed staffing with local supervision and HR administration, in select countries

Global Payroll

For your clients that already have a company in the country and stay the employer
  • Payroll processed across 175+ countries through one centralized platform, in multiple currencies, for employees and contractors.
  • Country-specific tax, statutory and compliance requirements managed, with accurate, on-time payroll and localized expertise.
  • Centralized reporting and payroll insights; payroll data integrated with HR and finance systems; scales as your clients expand.

Contractor Solutions

For your clients engaging independent contractors around the world

Contractor Management

  • Your client keeps the direct contractor relationship.
  • Streamlined onboarding and payments in local currencies.
  • W-8, W-9 and 1099 documentation and reporting.

Contractor of Record

  • PrismHR Global serves as the contractor of record.
  • Contractor agreements and compliant payments managed.
  • Reduced worker misclassification risk; country-specific contractor rules navigated.

Talent Solutions

For your clients who need to find the person first, or want a fully managed workforce

Talent Recruitment

  • Qualified candidates sourced across global talent markets, targeted by country, industry and role.
  • Faster recruiting and hiring timelines; a seamless move into Employer of Record when needed.

Talent Management

  • Everything in Talent Recruitment, plus workspace and housing arrangements where available.
  • Local HR administration and employee support; guidance on local employment practices.
