## Slide 5 — EOR is the better first move, and we say so

| Factors | EOR | Legal entity |
|---|---|---|
| Speed to first hire | **Fast**: employees can start without waiting for incorporation | **Slower**: Anywhere from 4-12 weeks normally, can be up to a year, depending on the country |
| Upfront work | **None**: no incorporation, registrations or bank account | Incorporation, tax and payroll registrations, local bank account |
| Ongoing cost and effort | Provider fee per employee; no entity administration | Accounting, tax filings and statutory compliance fees every year |
| Control | Provider is the employer of record | Company is the direct employer, with employment law and statutory liability |
| Best when | Testing a market, small teams, speed matters most | Strategic commitment to the country or a large local team |

Callout: "Both are right, at different stages. Our job is to help clients know when to switch."

## Slide 7 — What drives the need for an in-country presence? (1 of 2)

Lead panel: **As a business matures, an entity becomes the natural next step.** Customers want a
committed local presence:
- Local support
- Adaptation to language and culture
- Ability to contract locally, with tax and legal benefits

Four cases:
- **Development team** — Engineers who need a permanent local base
- **Manufacturing** — Production sites that hold assets and contracts locally
- **Customer support** — Support centers with local staff and equipment
- **Warehouse and distribution** — Hold inventory in-market and ship to customers

## Slide 8 — What drives the need for an in-country presence? (2 of 2)

- **Direct hiring** — Employees experience the company culture, get better benefits and can receive
  stock incentives.
- **Cost** — Past a certain team size, per-employee EOR fees can outweigh the cost of running an entity.
- **Acquisitions** — Acquiring a company that already has a legal entity: keep, merge or close it.
- **Legal exposure** — A large local team can create permanent establishment risk, which calls for a
  proper entity.

## Slide 14 — A seamless handoff from your EOR to their entity

1. **Client hires through Vensure's EOR** — The team starts work right away, no entity required.
2. **Cerity Global builds the entity** — Setup runs in parallel, typically 2 to 3 months, up to a year
   by country.
3. **Employees transition** — Moved from EOR to the new entity with no payroll or compliance gap.
4. **Cerity Global supports operations** — Ongoing HR, payroll, accounting, tax and compliance.

- **No disruption** — Your EOR keeps employees paid and compliant until the entity is live.
- **Client stays yours** — The relationship stays with you across every other market and service.
