## 0. The correction this round is built on

Rounds one, two and three were **instruments**: you tapped what you heard, the
app advanced, held state, and handed you the next line. A co-pilot driving the
call. The founder's direction, in three parts:

1. **The Playbook is a bank of responses with depth**, not an on-call response
   sidekick. A reference you go to. It runs nothing and tracks nothing.
2. **Depth is as deep as possible.** It cannot be too deep, and it has to be at
   your fingertips. How it layers down is the thing to iterate on — so that is
   what the three concepts vary.
3. **The country wing stays bolted on the left**, always available to the eye:
   type a country, see its details.

And from the round before: **owned and supported are never separated
client-facing.** No trace of that correction exists here.

## 1. What the bank is

**The unit is a response.** Something we say, with everything under it. Five
kinds, in the order a call runs:

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Open with** | The first line of a situation |
| **Ask** | A question we ask, one thought each, its reason attached |
| **They say** | Their line, realistic and hedged, and what we say back |
| **Explain** | A thing we lay out: a product, the fork, the list, the mechanism |
| **Close on** | The line that puts a next step on the calendar, and what it locks |

**Under every entry, as much depth as the record and the research hold:**

- **When** it applies — what they said, or the moment in the call
- **The fuller version** — the reasoning, the two-case explanation, what to say if they push
- **We do / they keep** — where the product's duty split matters
- **Say this** — a spoken line where one exists
- **Watch for** — the trap
- **Evidence** — every line with its rung: ON TAPE with the call date, FILED, RESEARCH with the source, ∅ where unproven
- **What comes next** — what they're likely to say, what we say then, and a link to the entry that continues it
- **Countries this touches** — chips that drive the wing

**Nine shelves, 85 entries.** Three reference, six situations — the founder's branches plus

**Find.** One field at the top of every shelf. Type what they said or what you
want to say; matches come back ranked, their line first, ours second, depth
third. The bank is big; the find is how it stays at your fingertips.

**The wing.** Unchanged from round three and confirmed: the can-we-even line
first, the lead lines, the sixteen behind one control, pins for the cluster of
countries a call names. Entries that touch a country carry chips that open it.

## 2. What varies — how depth layers down

**1 · The Fold** — depth opens in place under the row. One column of entries;
tap one and its whole depth unfolds beneath it. The shelf stays a list you can
scan. Closest to a paper battlecard you can expand.

**2 · The Drawer** — a compact, stable list in the middle; the full depth of the
selected entry in a right-hand drawer. Country left, entries center, depth
right. Tap another entry and only the drawer changes. A *what comes next* link
opens that entry in the same drawer.

**3 · The Page** — every entry is its own page. The shelf is a table of
contents; open an entry and it takes the center with all its depth as sections,
previous and next along the bottom, and every *what comes next* a link. The
most like a reference book.

Same bank, same wing, same find. The founder said the layering is what to
iterate on; these are three starting points for that.

## 3. What survived, and from where

Everything that was content in rounds one to three is in the bank as entries:
the questions and their answer rows, the true-regardless lines, the sourced
win-back facts, the product boundaries, the partner's own analogies, the
corrections. The win-back mechanism is an *Explain* entry on its shelf, not a
ledger you fill. The instruments — tapping, state, the relay list accumulating,
the give-back writing itself — are gone.

## 5. Open decisions

1. **The layering.** Which of the three, or which parts. This is the founder's
   stated iteration.
2. **Statutory figures on the wing.** Law is written in figures; the money
   doctrine is written for what we charge. Rendered as sourced; flagged.
3. **The risk map** is RESEARCH-rung and needs an operations pass before any of
   it is said on a call.
4. **Transfer-of-undertakings exposure** on an EOR takeover in Europe is marked
   ∅. Research owed.
5. **What's not on any shelf yet:** M&A, permanent-establishment risk from a
   sales hire, visas, exiting EOR back to an entity, a compliance scare. No tape
   yet; the shelves are there when it arrives.
