## 0. The correction this round is built on

Rounds one, two and three were **instruments**: you tapped what you heard, the
app advanced, held state, and handed you the next line. A co-pilot driving the
call. The founder's direction, in three parts:

1. **The Playbook is a bank of responses with depth**, not an on-call response
   sidekick. A reference you go to. It runs nothing and tracks nothing.
2. **Depth is as deep as possible.** It cannot be too deep, and it has to be at
   your fingertips. How it layers down is the thing to iterate on — so that is
   what the three concepts vary.
3. **The country wing stays bolted on the left**, always available to the eye:
   type a country, see its details.

And from the round before: **owned and supported are never separated
client-facing.** No trace of that correction exists here.

## 1. What the bank is

**The unit is a response.** Something we say, with everything under it. Five
kinds, in the order a call runs:

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Open with** | The first line of a situation |
| **Ask** | A question we ask, one thought each, its reason attached |
| **They say** | Their line, realistic and hedged, and what we say back |
| **Explain** | A thing we lay out: a product, the fork, the list, the mechanism |
| **Close on** | The line that puts a next step on the calendar, and what it locks |

**Under every entry, as much depth as the record and the research hold:**

- **When** it applies — what they said, or the moment in the call
- **The fuller version** — the reasoning, the two-case explanation, what to say if they push
- **We do / they keep** — where the product's duty split matters
- **Say this** — a spoken line where one exists
- **Watch for** — the trap
- **Evidence** — every line with its rung: ON TAPE with the call date, FILED, RESEARCH with the source, ∅ where unproven
- **What comes next** — what they're likely to say, what we say then, and a link to the entry that continues it
- **Countries this touches** — chips that drive the wing

**Nine shelves, 85 entries.** Three reference, six situations — the founder's branches plus
the ones agreed in review:

| Shelf | Entries | What's on it |
|---|---|---|
| How this works between us | 13 | Resale or referral, markup, the price-book posture, setup, the cost of teaching clients, funding, the deposit, the amendment, time to live, one screen, whose it is, what a quote needs |

**Find.** One field at the top of every shelf. Type what they said or what you
want to say; matches come back ranked, their line first, ours second, depth
third. The bank is big; the find is how it stays at your fingertips.

**The wing.** Unchanged from round three and confirmed: the can-we-even line
first, the lead lines, the sixteen behind one control, pins for the cluster of
countries a call names. Entries that touch a country carry chips that open it.

## 2. What varies — how depth layers down

**1 · The Fold** — depth opens in place under the row. One column of entries;
tap one and its whole depth unfolds beneath it. The shelf stays a list you can
scan. Closest to a paper battlecard you can expand.

**2 · The Drawer** — a compact, stable list in the middle; the full depth of the
selected entry in a right-hand drawer. Country left, entries center, depth
right. Tap another entry and only the drawer changes. A *what comes next* link
opens that entry in the same drawer.

**3 · The Page** — every entry is its own page. The shelf is a table of
contents; open an entry and it takes the center with all its depth as sections,
previous and next along the bottom, and every *what comes next* a link. The
most like a reference book.

Same bank, same wing, same find. The founder said the layering is what to
iterate on; these are three starting points for that.

## 3. What survived, and from where

Everything that was content in rounds one to three is in the bank as entries:
the questions and their answer rows, the true-regardless lines, the sourced
win-back facts, the product boundaries, the partner's own analogies, the
corrections. The win-back mechanism is an *Explain* entry on its shelf, not a
ledger you fill. The instruments — tapping, state, the relay list accumulating,
the give-back writing itself — are gone.

## 3b. Sixth round — the Ear

Founder review of round five, verbatim in substance: the Exchange's find field
is superficial because it only matches strings already on the page; typing
"asked before" did not surface the asked-before shelf; the field would only be
useful if it ran on inference — type what the person is saying and the app
thinks and hands back the best two or three options; the opened depth is
confusing; the Board is horrendous; the Manual sucks. Simplicity.

All three are retired. **Round six** is one face, not three, because the brief
narrowed to one idea and three versions of it would be costume:
`docs/mockups/playbook-face-the-ear-2026-09-11.html`.

**The Ear.** One field. Type what they're saying. Two or three cards come back:
a mono kicker naming the situation and the kind, and the line to say in serif.
Nothing else on arrival but the nine situations and reference shelves as plain
links under the field, for the moments before the call when you want to browse.

- **Open a card** and it reads out in one column: a quiet line on when it
  applies, the fuller version as plain paragraphs, then IF THEY SAY as chips
  carrying their likely words, then one source line with its rung and date
  (more sources behind a `+N more`). The other cards collapse to one line each
  so there is one place to look.
- **Click a chip** and their words become the field's text. The authored reply
  leads, already open, with its own fuller version, its own follow-ups and its
  own source; the brain fills the other two seats. That is the depth mechanism:
  the conversation continues through the one field, as far as the bank goes.
  `← back` restores the previous field and cards.
- **A country in what they type** lights the wing on the left the moment the
  name lands. Opening a card that touches countries pins them in the wing.
- **A situation link** fills the field with the shelf's label and shows its
  opener and next two by kind order; `N more` opens the rest of the shelf.
- **Nothing matches:** one plain line, the situations again. Under two content
  words the page stays quiet rather than flicker.

**The brain.** In this file the seat is a stand-in: a weighted index over every
field of the 85 entries plus the 120 follow-up lines (their words are prospect
lines too, and typed fresh they surface the authored reply), a domain thesaurus
of roughly forty concepts so loose speech lands ("using someone else" is an
incumbent, "farmed out" is win-back, "moved to" is the own-person shelf),
contraction handling, shelf-label matching so "asked before" leads with the
shelf that carries those words, and a two-or-three gate on relative score.
Nine entries carry a few alternate phrasings the stand-in needs and the model
never will. Thirty natural phrases from the tapes score 30/30 on first pick;
a looser second batch of twenty reads well with two soft spots noted below.

In production the seat is the model: the bank as a cached system prompt, the
typed text as the message, the answer a JSON list of two or three entry ids
and a country if one was named. The file carries that path now as a door at
the foot of the page: paste an Anthropic key and the Ear calls the model
directly from the browser on every pause in typing, with the stand-in showing
first and the model's picks replacing it when they land. The key stays in the
browser; nothing else leaves the page. Untested from the build environment,
which has no key.

## 5. Open decisions

1. **The layering.** Which of the three, or which parts. This is the founder's
   stated iteration.
2. **Statutory figures on the wing.** Law is written in figures; the money
   doctrine is written for what we charge. Rendered as sourced; flagged.
3. **The risk map** is RESEARCH-rung and needs an operations pass before any of
   it is said on a call.
4. **Transfer-of-undertakings exposure** on an EOR takeover in Europe is marked
   ∅. Research owed.
5. **What's not on any shelf yet:** M&A, permanent-establishment risk from a
   sales hire, visas, exiting EOR back to an entity, a compliance scare. No tape
   yet; the shelves are there when it arrives.
6. **Two bank gaps the Ear exposed (round six).** "I'm the wrong person for
   this, that's our sales team" has no entry, though the authoring canon names
   the case; and "we did the ASO versus PEO thing, is that basically it" lands
   on the wrong correction because the analogy lives only in an evidence line.
   Both are bank entries to write, not face changes.
7. **The model door** runs Claude Opus 5 at low effort with the bank cached in
   the system prompt and structured JSON out. Production would move the same
   call behind a server action so no key touches the browser. The stand-in's
   thesaurus and alternate phrasings are scaffolding for this file only.
