## 0. The founder's model, as built

The conversation opens on **what's their situation?** The country never drives the
talk track; it lives in a **prominent left panel** that reacts to any country they
name. Win-back gets its own situation and its own **mechanism** for reacting well
without the answer. Simplicity on arrival, depth beneath.

Two corrections from founder review are built in and the retired rounds carry
them no longer:

- **Owned and supported are never separated client-facing.** The line is *we
  operate there with our own entity in country*. No card, panel line or document
  in this round tells the seller to correct that. The internal coverage figure
  stays internal.
- **The face is situation-first, not lookup-first.** Round two opened on a search
  field. This round opens on six doors.

### The stage: what's their situation?

A **who's asking** row (their sales rep · their ops or payroll · their
leadership) that changes one operator-facing note on the opener, because a sales
rep's first question is *can I say yes and what's my margin* and an ops lead's
is *who does setup and who files*. Then six doors:

| Door | Opener (spoken) | The slots | The close locks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **They heard we do some global stuff** | Tell me what you've been hearing, and where it came up. | Where it came up · the four in their language · their own people | One-pager · a time theirs to cancel |
| **Clients have asked before. Nothing live today.** | Which asks do you remember? Even the ones you had to pass on. | What they did with the asks · anyone lost over it · who hears it first · their own people | Agreement sent · a slide for the reps · a time to walk the reps through it |
| **A client or prospect is asking right now** | Where are the people, and what did the client actually say they need? | Who employs them today (the fork) → the lane's two questions → the date | Three-way call · the list sent ahead |
| **Winning back work they farmed out** | Walk me through how it went out. Who's doing it now, and what changed? | Who does it now · what changed · the term · their paper · entity status → the mechanism | Questions sent today · a three-way call held |
| **Their own person moved abroad** | Where did they go, and are they still on your domestic payroll? | Still on US payroll · how long, and staying · right to work | Review call booked |
| **The client is on the call with them** | Tell me about the people. Where are they, and how are they paid today? | Who employs them · what's promised · the date | The partner holds the next step |

Every question carries one thought and its reason. Client-fact questions are
asked secondhand (*did the client say…*). Every question has a don't-know row,
and a don't-know goes onto the list for their client rather than stalling the
call. A row can hand off to another situation — *"a client asked, a while back"*
on door one lands you on door two; *"they're on another provider"* on door three
lands you on win-back. Every path ends on a give-back that reads back what was
tapped and asks *is that about right?*, then a close with a lock.

### The mechanism — for win-back, and under every situation

Four slots, filled live from what's been tapped:

- **What we know** — the filled slots.
- **What we don't** — the don't-knows.
- **Who finds out** — the partner asks the client, with the list. Then a three-way call.
- **True regardless** — the lines that hold whatever the answers turn out to be.

The true-regardless bank, with its rungs:

1. Opening a new entity is expensive everywhere and may not be needed. — ON TAPE 9/10
2. Paying people abroad from a US account is common, and it isn't compliant. — ON TAPE 9/10
3. Moving people to a new provider means the old one's notice, a new employment contract, and carrying over their leave and their start date. It runs longer than the first hire did. — RESEARCH
4. What a client calls contractor of record is our Contractor Plus. We hold the contract with the contractor and we classify them. — ON TAPE 7/7, RESEARCH
5. One place for everything is something you can offer and a standalone provider cannot. — ON TAPE 9/2

On the 9/10 partner call the seller ran this mechanism correctly without a card:
named the unknowns, offered two doors (a question list or joining the client
call), and gave one true thing regardless. The card writes down what he did.

## 2. The three concepts — how the stage moves

**1 · The Walk** — *one question at a time.* Pick the situation, then walk it.
The stage holds one question, its reason, and what they might say. Tap what you
heard; the next question arrives in the same spot. A thin crumb of slot-and-answer
rewinds. Strength: the calmest thing to look at during a call. Cost: you cannot
see what is coming.

**2 · The Sheet** — *the whole run sheet.* Pick the situation and the entire path
unrolls as one document, sections numbered, answers as lines you tick, branches
written as *→ §4* and tapping one scrolls you there. Give-back and close at the
bottom, the list as an appendix. Strength: you can see the whole call, and it
reads like the paper run sheets people already trust. Cost: it is long, and a
long page invites the eye to wander.

**3 · The Ledger** — *what we know, what we don't.* The win-back mechanism made
the stage for every situation. Each situation is a set of facts; the stage shows
them as slots and asks the one question that fills the next empty one. As slots
fill, the give-back and the client list write themselves; the four-slot card sits
underneath the whole time. Strength: the give-back is never assembled by hand,
and the don't-knows are never lost. Cost: a ledger reads as a form, and forms
change how a seller talks.

## 3. Research behind the win-back lines

- Switching employer-of-record providers: 30–90 day notice in the incumbent's
  agreement; the outgoing provider terminates and the new one issues a fresh
  compliant employment contract, typically effective the next business day;
  tenure preserved by recognising the original start date; accrued leave carried
  over from the outgoing provider's final statement; 4–8 weeks end to end.
  Sources: [People Managing People](https://peoplemanagingpeople.com/global-hr/switch-eor-provider/),
  [Deel](https://www.deel.com/blog/employer-of-record-switch-strategies/),
  [Borderless](https://www.hireborderless.com/post/employer-of-record-switch),
  [Justworks](https://www.justworks.com/blog/when-and-how-to-switch-employer-of-record-providers),
  [BIPO](https://www.biposervice.com/news/what-happens-to-my-employees-if-i-switch-eor-providers/).
- Contractor of record: the provider contracts directly with the contractor and
  carries classification, contracts, tax compliance and payment; agent of record
  leaves the client holding the contract. Terms are not standardised across
  providers. Sources: [Transformify](https://www.transformify.org/blog/contractor-management/agent-of-record-vs-contractor-of-record),
  [RemotePass](https://www.remotepass.com/blog/employer-of-record-vs-contractor-of-record),
  [Atlas](https://www.atlashxm.com/resources/what-is-a-contractor-of-record),
  [Deel](https://www.deel.com/blog/aor-vs-eor/).
- Sanctions: comprehensive programs on Cuba, Iran, North Korea and Syria (plus
  the occupied Ukrainian regions); targeted programs on Russia, Belarus,
  Venezuela and Myanmar among others. Sources: [MSU Export Controls](https://exportcontrols.msu.edu/sanctioned-and-embargoed-countries),
  [LegalClarity](https://legalclarity.org/ofac-countries-list-of-sanctioned-nations-and-restrictions/),
  [Sanctions Lawyers](https://sanctionslawyers.net/blog-en/ofac-sanctioned-countries-list-2026/).

A deeper pass is still owed on win-back: the incumbents' actual client
agreements, transfer-of-undertakings exposure in Europe, and what each competitor
means by contractor of record. The risk map is RESEARCH-rung throughout and needs
an operations pass before it is trusted on a call.

## 4. Named, not built

Scenarios surfaced in review that the tree does not carry as doors. Each is a
row or a note inside an existing door, or is left for a later round:

- **Relocation** (someone already on payroll moved) — folded into *their own
  person moved abroad* for the partner's own staff; a client's relocated employee
  enters through *a client is asking right now*.
- **Consolidation** (the client went to a provider directly) and **churn defense**
  (a client left over global) — rows inside win-back and door two respectively.
- **A competitor PEO doing it on our platform** — no door; a mechanism note is owed.
- **Legal-driver and cost-of-paying-driver** entries (IP, wire fees) — enter
  through door three's contractor lane; not modelled as their own doors.
- **Pricing-first**, **enablement for their reps**, **new people on a second
  call**, **the three-way call itself** — the last two are closes, not doors; the
  first two need a line on door two.
- **M&A, permanent-establishment risk from a sales hire, visas, exiting EOR back
  to an entity, a compliance scare** — no tape yet. Left for when there is.
