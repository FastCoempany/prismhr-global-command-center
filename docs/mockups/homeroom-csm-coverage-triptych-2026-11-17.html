Ask a CSM for a seat

One small object on the HomeRoom. It names one CSM and at most three of their accounts where the record holds nothing with you on it, and drafts your ask to join that CSM's next check-in for five minutes. The full list never shows. When you send the ask, the next CSM and the next three take its place. Nothing here ships until you give a ship order.

  • What counts as a touch. Anything on the record with you in it: an email you sent, received or were copied on, a call, meeting or transcript with you, a send you logged, and a colleague's email or Salesforce activity only when it carries your name or address. Support cases never count.
  • The real numbers today. Your book holds 149 accounts. 40 have a touch with you. 28 of those have a direct exchange; 10 only appear in your own emails to their CSM; 2 only in your own notes. 109 have nothing. By CSM: Lesha Cyphers 50, Anika Steenstra 43, Whitney Dideon 13, John Hebert 2, no CSM 1. By fit: 19 high, 25 medium, 65 low.
  • What it read. Every filed entry on every account in the database, all 125 accounts' slices of the Salesforce export, the sends you logged and your own touch log. It did not read the backup files on GitHub; they hold the same filings as whole files.
  • The order. An account whose record mentions the CSM's recurring meeting comes first, newest mention first, then by fit. 8 of the 109 carry a mention: bi-weekly, monthly or quarterly calls and business reviews in the CSM team's emails. A support case only supplies a mention when it talks about such a meeting; none of the 109 does today.
  • The counter. Business days since you last asked a CSM about accounts. The record's last one is 7/21, so today it reads red and pulses. At the threshold, five by default, it turns red; − and + change the threshold, ↺ restarts the count. Reduced motion keeps the red and drops the pulse.
  • The count is a door. "109 with nothing" opens to the split by CSM, never to the accounts. Picking a CSM there brings their top three forward.
  • Send. "Download for Outlook" downloads the email as an Outlook draft file and, on the same press, saves it on each named account's record. Those accounts now carry a touch with you, so the count drops and the next batch arrives. ✕ on an account sets it aside for today and pulls in that CSM's next one.
  • Sample. The accounts, CSMs, counts and mentions are real. The CSM addresses come from the record; John Hebert's is not on it. The draft's words are a first draft for you to change.
