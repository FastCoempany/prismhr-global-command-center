Who you've spoken to, by CSM

The CSM check-in seat, read as coverage. Every account in the book sits in one of four states, read from the whole record. Each CSM gets one counter that stays grey until their next check-in is five business days out, then turns amber and pulses, and one button drafts the ask to join that check-in for the accounts you haven't met. Nothing here ships until you give a ship order.

  • The four states. Spoken to: a call, a meeting or a transcript with you on it, or someone at the account writing to you. Written to, no answer: you emailed someone there and nobody there has written back. Only our side: a support case, the CSM's own activity or a colleague's email, and nothing with you. Nothing: no entry on either record.
  • The real numbers today. The book holds 149 accounts. You have spoken to someone at 15. You have written to 5 more with no answer. 84 have only our side on record: 48 of those are support cases alone, and 36 have the CSM's activity or a colleague's email. 45 have nothing at all. That leaves 134 accounts where you haven't had a conversation.
  • By CSM. Lesha Cyphers holds 66 accounts and you've spoken to someone at 7. Anika Steenstra holds 58, with 7. Whitney Dideon holds 19, with 1. John Hebert holds 3, Kathryn Maddox 2, and one account has no CSM. You've spoken to no one in those last six.
  • What it read. Every account note on the first record: emails, calls, meetings, transcripts, Teams and Salesforce lines, with the head line's sender and recipients. The second record: every row of the weekly Salesforce export, 125 accounts' worth, with who logged it and whether it carried you. Calendar acceptances, declines and auto-replies count for nothing, because they are machinery. Our side means the CSMs, Russ, Shane, Deana, Kellie, Aleks, Judi, Rick, Marcus, and any prismhr.com or vensure.com address.
  • What it did not read yet. The vault's files under accounts/ in git, which hold the same captures the notes were filed from, and the sendbook taps, which are your own outbound and would only add to the amber state. Both go in before this ships.
  • The ask. One email per CSM: may I join your next check-in with these accounts for five minutes. One press downloads the email for Outlook with the files you ticked, and saves it to the record on every account it names. Those accounts then read SEAT ASKED with the day, in blue, until a meeting or a reply moves them to spoken to.
  • The counter. Business days to the CSM's next check-in. The check-in days below are SAMPLE; the record does not hold the CSMs' calendars, so you set each one with "change" and it remembers. Reset puts the next date one cadence ahead of the last.
