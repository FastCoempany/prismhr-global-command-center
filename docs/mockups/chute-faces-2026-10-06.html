Call 1 · part two

The receipt

  • One line per filing at both doors: the account, what it filed, the day, and how it was routed. That is what your D12 ruling says a receipt keeps.
  • A second line shows only when something needs saying: the read was cut, the duplicate check didn't run, or the reader was down.
  • The line opens to what the filing wrote. ↺ takes the whole filing back. ✕ clears the receipt.
  • Today's long line, with its dash aside and its brackets, goes. So do the Drop's opened-action chips: the to-dos show in TODAY, which springs open on filing.
