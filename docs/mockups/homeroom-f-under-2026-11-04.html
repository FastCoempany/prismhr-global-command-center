Concept F, Under the Sentence

Updated 11/4: every deal now rests as its sentence alone. The ▸ before the sentence opens everything else, and Open all in the HomeRoom bar opens every deal at once. When a deal is open, F7, F8 and F9 show three ways to lay out what sits under the sentence.

You chose the original F and asked me to fix the area under each sentence. In F, that area held the client's confidence, a line of small chips and the next move, all in small grey text, with a lot of empty space to the right. These three versions keep F's sentence and map exactly as they were. They remove what repeats the sentence and give everything else its own labeled place in larger type.

Three versions of F · waiting on your pick · nothing ships without your order
  • Removed because the sentence already says it. The shape chip, such as "EOR +GP", and the line after it, such as "RESALE · INDIA", are gone from every row. The sentence names the products, reseller or referral, and the countries. The shape chip used to open what the read stands on; hovering any fact in the sentence shows the same thing.
  • Brought forward. Each of these now has a label and full-size text instead of a small chip:
    • how sure the record is about the client, and the email or call that says so
    • who is in the deal: the MULTI badge, the number of people, and their names
    • whether the partner is briefed
    • the last research pass, what it found when you hover it, and a button to run it again
    • the deal's stage, which was only inside More before
    • Salesforce, as a button that opens the record
    • the confirmed fact from your weekly Salesforce export, which was only inside More before, now written as a sentence under the next move
  • Kept together. The next move, Mark it done, the files folder, Send files and ⚡ paste stay side by side. More still opens the latest email in full, the Drop, EARLIER and the stage's full meter, and it now also holds the button to add a country by hand.
  • The same as F otherwise. The map, the sample deals, the one pulsing light on the nearest client date, the demos and the other kept parts are the same as in F. The account names come from your book. Every fact is invented for the sample SAMPLE, including the names of the people in each deal and the research notes. There are no money figures.

Version F7

One row of labeled facts

  • What it is. Under the sentence, one white bar runs the full width of the row. It is split into six labeled cells: the client, the people in the deal, the partner, research, the stage and Salesforce. Under the bar, the next move and its buttons sit on one line, with the Salesforce fact under the move.
  • What it costs. On a narrow screen the six cells wrap into two rows of three.
Version F8

Tiles

  • What it is. Under the sentence, each piece of information is its own small white tile with an icon, a label and its value. The next move is a large tile at the left, with Mark it done inside it. The other tiles are the client, the people in the deal, the partner, research, the stage, the files sent, Salesforce and the latest email.
  • What it costs. It is the tallest of the three, and every tile looks equally important, so the next move stands out only by its size.
Version F9

The move on the left, the facts on the right

  • What it is. Under the sentence, the left side holds the next move, its buttons, the Salesforce fact and a preview of the latest email: its subject and its first two lines. The right side is a list of labeled facts, one per line: the client, the people, the partner, research, the stage and Salesforce.
  • What it costs. The email preview makes each row taller. In return the latest email sits right next to the move it is usually about.
