The HomeRoom with every new object in it

Five objects from the last two days, placed on one HomeRoom page: Write, Ask Russ, the seat ask, the Judi handoff and Teams channels. The live room stays as it ships today, drawn small: the country map, one sentence per deal, the opened row's ladders, NEXT MOVE, the people deck and the one door. The three concepts put the same objects in three different places. Nothing ships until you give a ship order.

  • Concept A, on the row. Ask Russ sits on the silent deal's row and the Judi handoff sits on the Closed Won row. Write, Seats and Teams are quiet buttons beside + add, each opening a panel under the header. The trade-off: the page-wide tools are out of sight until you press them.
  • Concept B, the desk. A column on the right holds Write, Seats and Teams stacked, and stays in view while the rows scroll. Rows still carry their own Ask Russ and Judi objects. The trade-off: the rows lose a third of the width all the time.
  • Concept C, one strip. One quiet strip under the header holds a counter per object: deals past five days, the CSM batch, handoffs waiting, new lines from Teams. Each counter opens its panel in place, and rows carry only a small mark that opens the same panel. The trade-off: every object is one click away, and none is ever open on arrival.
  • What all three share. Every draft downloads an Outlook file and saves to the account's record on one press, with a receipt. Orange appears only on the download or send button inside an open draft. Mark it done stays green. The library carries what shipped on 11/18: the two whole demos with their start times, five one-pagers, two agreement review copies, and any file from your computer.
  • Sample data. The deal sentences, dates and drafts are samples. The coverage counts are real: 109 of 149 accounts have nothing on the record that involves you.
