HomeRoom overhaul · Judi handoff · Triptych · 2026-11-17

Hand a signed deal to Judi

One object on a deal row. It stays dark until you mark the deal Closed Won, then it lights up with what Judi needs to take the account and the five early adopter calls. Three structures for the same object. Nothing here ships until you give a ship order.

  • What lights it. Your ☑ Closed won on the row, or the board's Closed Won. Press "☑ Closed won" on any row below to see it light. ↺ on the closed stamp puts it back to dark.
  • What it holds. The PEO and client contacts, the countries, the headcount, employees or contractors, the start date, the client's first payroll date, what was promised during the sale, and where support goes. Reseller partners open a CRC ticket. Referral clients use the help button inside the app. Each fact names where it came from. A fact the record does not hold says so and takes your hand.
  • The calls. The internal outline's five calls. Judi leads 1 and 5 onward. You lead 2, 3 and 4. Calls 1 and 2 date from the day implementation completes. Calls 3 and 4 date from the client's first payroll. The spacing is a suggestion you can change: call 2 six days after implementation, call 3 three days before payroll, call 4 two days after, call 5 a month after call 4. Calls 2 to 4 carry a reminder to read the feedback form first.
  • The one move. "Send Judi the handoff" drafts the email, with Danielle Stansberry as an optional copy and files from the whole library. One press downloads the Outlook draft and saves it to the account's record. In this mockup the save is shown as a receipt and the draft carries file names, not the files.
  • The Judi mark. A blue line marks the facts Judi most needs to hear from you, with the reason.

Concept A

The card that unfolds on the row

  • Shape. A dashed strip sits under the row's move while the deal is open. Closed Won turns it into a card on the row itself: the facts on the left, the calls on the right, the move at the foot.
  • Good for. Seeing everything at once on the deal you just closed. Cost. The row gets tall while the card is open.

Concept B

The timeline band

  • Shape. Closed Won draws a band across the row from signing to the ongoing check-ins. Each call is a stop with its date and who leads it; filled blue stops are yours. Clicking a stop opens its agenda. The facts sit in a strip under the band.
  • Good for. Seeing when you are next on and what each call is for. Cost. The facts are smaller, and the band scrolls sideways on a phone.

Concept C

The checklist sheet

  • Shape. The row keeps one line, "Hand it to Judi", with a count of what is missing. The line opens a sheet on the right edge: a checklist of each fact, ticked when the record holds it, then each call with its date and its leader, then the email.
  • Good for. Working down what is missing before the handoff goes. Cost. The handoff is one click away from the row instead of on it.

Sample data throughout. Chassie Smith, Simploy's COO, and the India contractors come from the record's July notes; the engagement, the promise and every date are made up to show the behavior.
