Concept A

The letter

  • The stage is the note. Action and reason on top, the draft beneath, edited in place. Send is the one orange move.
  • The waiting wing shows which moves are drafted. Click a name to put its draft on the stage.
  • The chips fold under "Why this note". Each one says what it means and whether the draft uses it.
  • Costs: one account at a time, as today. The stage gets taller.
Concept B

The run

  • The send band becomes a run through a stack of drafts. One card, keyboard driven: S sends, E edits, N skips to tomorrow.
  • The wings turn into a run log. Sent on the left with each one's loop state, still to go on the right.
  • The pip bar reads the run at a glance: amber sent, green seen in Sent Items, ink is now.
  • Costs: the queue's heat map flattens into run order. Moves outside the send band need their own run.
Concept C

The cited draft

  • Every sentence shows what it stands on. A numbered mark beside it, the evidence in the margin. Hover either to match them.
  • The chips survive as sources, not badges. What the draft leaves out is listed too, with the reason.
  • Evidence or nothing: a sentence with no source cannot be drafted.
  • Costs: a busier stage. The margin drops under the draft on narrow screens.
