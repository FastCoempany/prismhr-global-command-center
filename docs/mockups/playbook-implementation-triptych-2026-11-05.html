A run of show for the first implementation call.

One new space in the Playbook, beside the products. It holds what happens on the first call after a partner signs: who is on it, what gets covered in what order, what has to be true before it starts, and what everyone owes after it ends. The first one on tape ran 39 minutes and is the starter for all three. Same content in each. What changes is how it is organized: by time, by who owns what, or by the date the partner promised their client.

Organized by time. The call in the order it runs. Thirteen parts down the left, each with who leads it and when it ran on tape. The open part reads in the middle: what to cover, the question to ask and why, what to listen for, and anything still undecided. Before and after sit on the right as checklists. Best for running the call live.

Organized by owner. Who owns what, before, during and after. Four rows for the four people on the call, three columns for the three phases. Your row is lit. Open any card to read its detail below the board. Best for making sure nothing falls between the seller and the account manager.

Organized by the date. Start from the day the partner promised their client a first payment, and work back. Change the date and every milestone moves: the call, the agreement, the invites, the approval, the payment. The call is one stop on the way. The dates are estimates built from what was said on tape and are marked that way. Best for telling a partner, on the call, whether their date holds.
