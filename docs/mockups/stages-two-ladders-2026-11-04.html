Two Stage Ladders

The stages become two ladders. The partner ladder runs once for each PEO and follows the relationship and the agreement: from not met, through signing and the first call after signing, to live. The client ladder runs once for each client a PEO brings and follows that deal from named to live. These three versions show the same ladders and the same sample book in three different places.

Three versions · waiting on your pick · nothing ships without your order
  • The partner ladder. Not met, Engaged · cold, Engaged · warm, Reviewing, Signed, Oriented, Live. Live is read from the client ladder: a partner is live when one of its clients is live, and nobody sets it by hand.
  • The client ladder. Named, Scoped, Shown, Won, Onboarding, Live. Lost and Passed stay as the two ways out.
  • Hover any stage to see where it came from. Each stage names the email, call, calendar event or send that put it there. Click any step on a ladder to set the stage by hand; a stage you set says so and has ↺ to go back to what the record says.
  • The signed partners are the point of this page. Simploy, Infiniti HR and Marvel HR have all signed and have no client live, each for a different reason. Each version has to make those three easy to tell apart.
  • The sample. The account names come from your book. Simploy's client, Everest Transportation and Packaging, and today's call with Judi Owen are as you told me. Every other fact is invented for the sample SAMPLE. There are no money figures.

Version L1

In the HomeRoom row

  • What it is. Each account keeps its HomeRoom row. The left side holds the name, the agreement kind and today's move. The right column holds the partner ladder on top and one client ladder for each client underneath. A signed partner with no client shows an amber line where the client would be.
  • What it costs. You see the ladders one account at a time. Nothing shows the whole book at once, so you cannot see at a glance how many partners are stuck after signing.
Version L2

The board

  • What it is. One column for each step of the partner ladder, with each partner as a card in its column. Each card lists its clients with a small client ladder. The Signed and Oriented columns are underlined in amber, because the work there is getting a first client live. Click a card to open both ladders and their sources under the board.
  • What it costs. The client ladder is small on the cards, so a client's stage is easier to read in the drawer than on the board. A column holds every partner at that step, so a busy column gets long.
Version L3

The grid

  • What it is. The partner ladder runs down the side and the client ladder runs across the top. Each partner sits in one cell: its partner stage, and its furthest client's stage. The amber cells are partners that signed and have no client live yet, so Simploy, Infiniti HR and Marvel HR sit in three different amber cells. Click a name to open both ladders under the grid.
  • What it costs. A partner with two clients sits in its furthest client's column, and its other client shows only as "+1" until you open it. The grid is wide, so on a phone it scrolls sideways inside its box.
