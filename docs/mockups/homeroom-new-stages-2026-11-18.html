HomeRoom · A new start · Deals by stage · 2026-11-18

Every deal in the stage it has reached

The HomeRoom becomes a board along the partner ladder. Each deal is a card that moves right as it progresses: not met, engaged cold, engaged warm, reviewing, then signed onward. A card says three things only: the account, the one move, and where their client stands. Everything else is one click down.

  • The columns. The partner ladder's seven stages, with signed, oriented and live sharing the last column so the board fits. The card in that column says which of the three it is.
  • The two ladders stay honest. The board is by the partner ladder. The client's stage is one quiet line on the card, and both ladders show in full when the deal opens.
  • Tools sit on the card they belong to. Ask Russ shows on a deal that has gone quiet. The Judi handoff shows on a signed deal. A card carries at most one of these marks.
  • Write, Seats and Teams live in three quiet words at the top right. Each opens in the same place a deal opens, so only one thing is ever open and the board itself never carries them.
  • Setting a stage by hand. When the record is behind, set the stage from the open deal. It reads YOUR MARK, and ↺ puts back what the record shows. In the first variation you can also drag a card to another column.
  • Sample data throughout. The accounts are real names from the book. People, dates, counts and phone numbers are made up to show the behavior.
Frame

Variation A

Columns, and a panel at the side

  • How it works. Five columns, cards stacked in each. A card opens its deal in a panel that slides over the right edge. The whole board stays in view behind it. Trade-off: at half width the columns scroll sideways inside the board.

Variation B

Stages as rows

  • How it works. Each stage is a row, its deals laid across it. A card opens its deal in place, directly under its own row, at full width. Trade-off: the board reads top to bottom instead of left to right, so progress runs down the page.

Variation C

The stage strip, one stage at a time

  • How it works. One strip across the top shows the five stages and how many deals sit in each. One stage opens below as a list, with the chosen deal beside it. It opens on the stage that needs you, marked with a red dot. Trade-off: you see one stage's deals at a time, not the whole board.
