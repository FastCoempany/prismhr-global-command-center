The intent read · ten minutes · due when yesterday's expires

What the grab is, and how the ten minutes go.

What it is

A one-click capture button — ▤ Grab Sales Nav intent — that lives on your bookmarks bar. You installed it once from the app's Capture page by dragging it up. It is not an integration and it never talks to LinkedIn on its own: it fires only on Sales Navigator, only when you click it, and reads only the list you opened.

The ten minutes

1 · Open Sales Navigator's Accounts list — your 118 uploaded accounts.

2 · Click ▤ on your bookmarks bar. It walks the whole list for you — scrolling to load every row and clicking through every page, collecting names, intent levels, activity counts, and alerts as it goes. All 118 takes under a minute; keep the tab in front. When it finishes, one navy button copies everything and opens the paste target.

3 · Paste. The app reads the rows, matches them to your book, and shows you the proposed signals — "Amplify: High, 11 activities, 7/30" — which file when you confirm and re-rank this queue. The paste target is the Intranet's capture box today; it becomes this room's own drop when the room ships.

Why it nags when due, not when done

Readings expire after seven days, and the queue refuses to rank on a stale one. So the room stays silent when a fresh read is in — the work speaks through the rankings — and shows the one quiet line you clicked only when the next read is due.
