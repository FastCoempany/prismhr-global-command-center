Today, in order

The HomeRoom becomes the short list of what to do today, drawn from every deal and put in the order to do it. Deals stay in the background: a deal opens only when you click its name or the deal list. Three structures of the same idea follow.

  • What makes the list. A reply or a promise owed, a deal silent five business days or more (Ask Russ), a deal you marked Closed Won (the Judi handoff), a CSM seat to ask for, and a client date coming up. A move left from yesterday returns at the top and says so.
  • Where Write, Seats and Teams went. They sit on one quiet line under the list, because they are tools you reach for, not today's moves; when a seat is due, the ask itself joins the list as an item.
  • One orange at a time. Only the item or tool you have open shows its orange move. Mark it done is green. Every item can be done or put off for today, and both come back with ↺.
  • Sample data from the record's shapes: the accounts and people are real names from the book; the dates, counts and drafts are made up to show the behavior.
Frame

Variation A

The list, worked top to bottom

  • How it reads. One column of today's moves. The first one is open with its draft and its buttons; the rest are one line each. Done moves leave the list and wait in "Done today" at the foot.
  • Good for. Seeing the whole day at once and picking any move. Cost. With a long day, the list grows taller than one screen.

Variation B

One move at a time

  • How it reads. One move fills the room, open, with its draft. Under it, only the next move's line and how many are left. The full queue opens one click down.
  • Good for. Working through the day without being pulled sideways. Cost. You see the rest of the day only when you ask for it.

Variation C

The day in three bands

  • How it reads. The day's moves sit in the three bands the app already keeps: sends from 9 to 11, people from 11 to 2, and prep and filing from 2. The band you are in is open; the others fold to one line with their count. A send left over from the morning stays in its band and says so.
  • Good for. Doing the right kind of work at the right hour. Cost. A move in a later band is one click away until its hour.

Sample data throughout. The file names are real; the drafts download as Outlook files that list their attachments by name.
