Three versions · waiting on your pick · nothing ships without your order
  • In all three, people in the deal is a deck. One person is up front as a full card. The cards behind show only a tab with the person's initials, in their colored ring, and their first name.   • The card to the right shows the person up front: their name with a LinkedIn mark, their full title, their email, office phone, mobile and location, and every exchange with them. No LinkedIn on file shows the mark in a red circle with a line through it, and anything missing says "Not on file yet". A vertical line separates the deck from this card.

The fanned hand

  • What it is. The cards behind fan out to the right like a hand of playing cards, each one turned a little more. Each shows its tab on the right edge, with the first name running down the side.
  • What it costs. With many people the fan gets wide, so past four or five it needs ‹ and › more than the tabs.
Version F27

The stack

  • What it is. The cards behind stack upward like a pile of index tabs, each rising a little above the one in front of it. Each shows its tab along its top edge, with the initials and first name reading across, so the names read the normal way.
  • What it costs. The stack grows taller with each person, so the section is tallest when the deal has the most people.
Version F28

The flow

  • What it is. The person up front sits in the middle, and the others sit on both sides, turned away from you and smaller, like album covers on a shelf. Each shows its tab on the outer edge. Pressing › brings the next person to the middle.
  • What it costs. It takes the most width of the three, and on a phone it shrinks to fit.
