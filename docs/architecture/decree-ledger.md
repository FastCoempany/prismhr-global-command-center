# Decree Ledger

Audit pass 3, taken on main at 3726363 on 2026-09-24, after the bug pass. CLAUDE.md is the canon (542 lines at that commit); the code is what runs. This pass measures the distance between them in both directions: which decrees the code honors and how, which nothing enforces, and which ingest behaviors no decree governs.

Method: seven readers took the sections in parallel (the Chute; ingest copy against the writing canon and plain-speech law; the Ted doctrine and closer rule; the second record with the meat law and vehicle rule; Groundwork, the Sendbook and the Act Lane; the Spring, Scratchpaper and click-depth law; a violations-only sweep of the design canon, playbook authoring canon, direct doctrine and Playbook face). The Chute core, roomPaste, the misfile guard, the routing roster, the queue caps, the Act Lane's send path and the Accounts sheet's board read were read by hand as well, and every claim two readers disagreed on was re-read. Every cite is against main at 3726363. [pass-1 cite] means the line was taken from the map and not re-checked; [inferred] means the claim comes from code shape, not a run. Decrees are quoted verbatim with their CLAUDE.md line; where a quote is cut for length the cut is marked with an ellipsis and the line range covers the whole sentence.

Enforcement classes, strictly: TEST names the suite, the title and the assertion, says whether it pins BEHAVIOR (calls the function) or TEXT (reads the source as a string), and whether the suite runs in the verify chain (package.json:10 `test`, 41 suites) or by hand only. CONSTRUCTION cites the type, schema or data shape that makes the violation impossible to write; a comment, a bypassable helper, or a filter one page applies is not construction. HONOR means nothing stops the code from violating it.

Pass 1's three contradictions, confirmed on this tree: "every tab re-derives" (CLAUDE.md:283) while the Intranet mirrors only on a human-driven sweep (src/app/intranet/runners.ts:146-151) and roomPaste's refresh() names five paths (src/app/room/actions.ts:65-71) with nothing under src/lib/activity or src/app/activity revalidating anything; "the same pipeline a paste takes" (:281) while the .csv forks out of roomPaste at src/app/room/chute.tsx:356-359, the Drop reads one file (src/app/room/room-client.tsx:594-596) and the Intranet's Send-it writes intranetDoc rows directly [pass-1 cite, src/app/intranet/actions.ts:103-245]; the misfile guard's contradicting comments are gone since PR #329 and their absence is pinned by tests/ingest-defects.test.ts:81-87.

Two corrections to the earlier passes. The pass-2 ledger's A10 row says the Accounts sheet reads PeoState raw at src/app/accounts/page.tsx:411-412; it does not. Those lines read rows from loadCommand (:111-112), which liftForBoard lifts at src/lib/command-center/data.ts:59-64 and :91-107, and the only other reader, src/app/accounts/draft-actions.ts:57-62, lifts by hand.
