# Pass 14 — the nine left on honor

Pass 13 left nine rows on honor. Pass 14 walked each one, moved one to chain, widened the click-depth sweep to a thirteenth face, closed two holes in the canon lint, and weighed one idea for the four wording rows before setting it aside (§3). Eight stay, each with its reason below.

## 1. What moved

| Row | Decree | Now | Pin | What it took |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A12.3 | "The reason is the trigger, not a description … Recency alone is never a reason." | chain | tests/ingest-guard.test.ts › "every reason on the ingest surfaces names the fact it stands on (A12.3, whole scope)" (2); tests/ingest-faces.test.ts › "the receipt's second line names the fact it stands on (A12.3, whole scope)" | On the ingest surfaces a reason is one of three things, and each is pinned. The held box's rule reason is built from the rung's own why, so it names the evidence it stands on: the address, the contact, the quoted word, the initials or the read, for every why the router can produce. The model's reason passes the nine-word cap and the canon's lint or the rule's reason stands, and the lint now kills recency ("recently", "lately", "a while") and the hedges "may be", "might be" and "probably", which it let through before. The receipt's second line speaks only for a cut read with its counts, a skipped duplicate check or a reader that was down, each a fact with its figure or its name. |

## 2. What widened

- **A5.1 to A5.4.** The Playbook's Sheet is mounted: the click-depth sweep, the budget ratchet and the palette sweep read it on arrival, thirteen faces now. Its five doors and the between-us foot pass as they stand.
- **The canon lint.** `lintReason` carries the recency kill, so the model's reason gate and every gem reason take it, not only the ingest copy sweep. `HEDGE_RE` gains "may be", "might be" and "probably": the verdict's reason gate accepted "Advocate Pay may be a different company than Regis." before this pass.

## 3. One idea weighed and set aside

The four wording rows (A12.1, A12.10, A12.12 and, before this pass, A12.3) are judgment: whether a line is imperative, talks about history as a property, or carries a constructed phrase. A register of every operator string on the ingest surfaces, each read by hand and listed, with the chain refusing any string not on it, would have moved them the way the surfaces and mockups registries moved A2.8 and A6.12. The sweep enumerates about 830 strings across 53 files in scope, most of them prompts, keys and heads no operator reads. A register that size, approved in one pass, is a stamp, not a read. It was not built. The rows stay, with the lints they have.
