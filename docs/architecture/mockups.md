# Mockups Registry

Every mockup under docs/mockups has one row here. The row says what the document judged and what became of it: shipped on a recorded order, a concept that never shipped, or a document from before the rule. tests/canon/standing-decrees.test.ts reads this table: a mockup with no row, a row naming a file that is gone, or a disposition that is not one of the three fails the chain. "Mockup work NEVER ships without an explicit ship order" (CLAUDE.md, The Spring, founder-decreed 2026-08-19): a mockup's disposition is written here before its winner reaches production, and a shipped row names its order.

The ship-order rule was decreed 2026-08-19. A mockup judged before that day predates the rule and says so.
